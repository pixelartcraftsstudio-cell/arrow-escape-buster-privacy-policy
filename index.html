Effective Date: November 2, 2026

We may collect limited information needed to run and improve the game. This may include:

  • Device type and operating system version

We use the information we collect to:

Your game progress, scores, achievements, and settings may be stored on your device so the game can save your progress and preferences.

If you uninstall the game, local data may be removed from your device depending on your device settings.

Arrow Escape Buster may use third-party services such as Google Play Services, analytics providers, or crash reporting tools. These services may collect technical information about your device and how the app is used.

Arrow Escape Buster is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

6. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information we collect. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Choices

You can control or delete game data by:

  • Resetting the game from your device settings

If you contact us through email or support, you may also request that your information be deleted.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make significant changes, we will post the updated policy in the game or on our website, if available.

Your continued use of the game after changes are made means you accept the updated policy.
